## 🗺️ Practical Guide to Finding Toilets in Tokyo

### 🚉 1. Check the nearest station first

Most Japanese train stations have toilets.

This includes:

* JR stations
* Tokyo Metro stations
* Toei Subway stations
* Private railway stations

If you suddenly need a toilet, the nearest station is often one of the easiest options.

---

### 🏪 2. Convenience store toilets are not always available

Whether you can use a convenience store toilet depends on the individual store.

Common situations include:

* Open to everyone
* Ask staff before using
* Customers only
* Toilet unavailable or not provided

Therefore, convenience stores should not be considered a guaranteed option.

---

### 🏬 3. Shopping malls and department stores are reliable options

When shopping, sightseeing, or caught in the rain, look for:

* Department stores
* Large shopping malls
* Commercial complexes
* Electronics stores

These places generally have multiple toilets and relatively good facilities.

---

### 🌳 4. Public parks

Many parks in Tokyo have public toilets.

When walking around areas such as Shibuya, Harajuku, Yoyogi, or Ebisu, try searching for:

`公衆トイレ`

or simply:

`トイレ`

---

### 📱 5. Search on Google Maps

Try searching Google Maps for:

`トイレ`

`公衆トイレ`

or:

`toilet`

You can then choose the closest available toilet.
